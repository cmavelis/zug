### Part 1: Block the opponent score
- Assuming the opponent is trying to score, the only way that can happen is with their 2 pushing their 5 North.
- To prevent the 5 from advancing, push where it will end up with your 3

### Part 2: Score your own piece
- Look at the pieces your opponent can place, 1 and 3.
- These will both be placed before your 4 and 5 can act, so you can safely try to move your 4 in.
- In case your 4 gets blocked, push South with your 5 as well, which will get the 1 or 3 out of the way, and your 4 in the goal!

### Notes
- This is not the only solution or a guaranteed outcome for this board state.
- It all depends on what your opponent decides to do!
