## Overview

- Combine **move** and **push** actions to advance your pieces
- Your actions happen at the same time as your opponent's. Anticipate to come out ahead!
- Get your pieces to the opponent's side **4 times** to win!

## 1. Your turn

- On your turn, you must assign an action to each of your pieces
- There are 4 main actions:
  - Move straight <span class="pi pi-arrow-up" />
  - Move diagonal <span class="pi pi-arrow-up-right" />
  - Push straight <span class="zi zi-arrow-up-flat" />
  - Push diagonal <span class="zi zi-arrow-up-right-flat" />
- A **move** will try to move your piece in that direction. If blocked, nothing happens
- A **push**ing piece won't move, but pushes away pieces in the chosen direction

## 2. Turn resolution

- You and your opponent are deciding on your actions at the same time, in secret!
- Once you each decide on your actions, and have ended your turn, the actions are **resolved** using a **priority** system
- Pieces act in **priority** order, indicated by their number
  - Pieces with a 1 go first, then 2, etc.
- Actions from two pieces with the same **priority** happen in order of the _action_'s priority. Here is the action priority order:
  - <span class="pi pi-arrow-up" /> Move straight
  - <span class="zi zi-arrow-up-flat" /> Push straight
  - <span class="pi pi-arrow-up-right" /> Move diagonal
  - <span class="zi zi-arrow-up-right-flat" /> Push diagonal
  - <span class="pi pi-download" /> Place
- Use priority to your advantage and to create winning combos!

## 3. Replacing pieces

- When your piece ends the turn off the board or in the scoring zone, it is removed.
- The **place** <span class="pi pi-download" /> action is used to replacing any missing pieces on your turn, to bring you back up to 4
- **Placing** a piece on top of another piece will remove both immediately!
