### To score 2 points, use each numbered piece like this: 
1. Place anywhere but on the 2
2. Push the 3, which causes a chain reaction, pushing the 4 into the goal
3. Move South or Southeast into the goal.
4. Don't move! Use a straight push in order to stay still in the goal. The trick here: you only score for pieces that are still in the goal at the end of the turn.
